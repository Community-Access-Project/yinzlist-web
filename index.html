Create your own events

A run on Tuesday, a swim on Friday, a picnic at the park — to your friends, to a group, or to the whole city. No ads, no short videos, no distractions. Just the community you are already part of.

  • No advertising.
  • No tracking and no analytics.
  • No location permission.
  • No cost, now or later.

Local resources

Federal, state, and local communities pay out food help, heating help, health coverage, child care and tax credits to people who apply, and every year a great deal of it goes unclaimed by people who qualify and never knew it existed.

Food
SNAP · WIC · Food pantries · COMPASS
Heat, phone and bills
